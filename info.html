ApiconIASIS

The world's bees are in danger from a mysterious disease.

Determined to help, Flora worked in her laboratory and created a special healing enzyme using beneficial bacteria.

After growing the bacteria and carefully isolating the enzyme, she developed a potential treatment that could help protect bee populations.

Now Flora must travel around the world, meet different bee species, and deliver the cure before it is too late.

Every bee 🐝 saved brings nature one step closer to recovery.
